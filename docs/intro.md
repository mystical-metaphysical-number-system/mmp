Thus it is a good time to note that MMP is neither a finitist nor an infinitist philosophy, nor a purported third position. It is a Rosetta stone, a keystone between two mathematical canons which have withstood the sands of time and reached the orbits of the moon. It is the unfortunate circumstance that the majority of readers herein come almost exclusively from the real number canon — thus may demand additional justification and explanation toward what finitists would find immediately apparent. Neither approach is promoted at the others' expense, but meticuously clarifies the distinction and domain between the two through its model.

MMP begins by asking what *the nothingness* actually represents. One may immediately reach for their conception of zero, they remark an absense of something, a placeholder.  They may be content with the phrase "I have zero things", however here the philosopher and logician would attest the inconsistency- one has a something to describe a nothing, you cant hold zero things, so can we assign units to 0?  Absolute zero in physics still contains a degree of freedom, that half quantum of energy. if no system can be without positive energy than a statement of zero joules must be in the context of an equality, which can easily be represesented without zero (x+y=0 or x = -y).  One may than reason that, while *the nothingness* and zero are usefull concepts in their own domains, this implicit equality of the two dont actually hold water.

MMP continues from another direction, what is the *the infinite*?  One may immediately reach for their conception of infinity- something immesurably large, long or heavy.  In many instances, such as the magnetic field around a wire, such a reasoning helps in the understanding of nature.  However the logician again points out the inconsistency.  While they may conceptualize the object, they would ask them, where is it? they could provide a start but no end.  Indeed, when pondering the matter, if waldo was infinitely long, you'd have no trouble finding where the mascot may begin, but you can never confirm or prove thats where waldo is, you'd maybe see his shoes but the final confirmation, the tip of this hat, you'll never see. So this idea, that infinity can be place on the numberline, like it has a beginning and end someplace far away is also unreasonable, and from here one begins to feel the contrasting nature of the *the nothingness* and *the infinite*.

One may reason that these ideas are opposition orientations on the same pole.   Zero has endless precision and no length; infinity has endless length at no precision, neither belong on any numberline by themselves.  MMP than, in its determination to fracture modernities dominant notion of the continuum, is obliged to replace it with something it finds more teneble. Perhaps than a continuum can be modelled from a sort of congealing of poles, a balancing of opposites- a quaternay so that ones reasonings about *the nothingness* and the *the infinite* remain congruent between their logics and their maths- MMP defines *the infinite nothingess* - oriented voids that donuts in and out of itself, as the defined fundemental element of the Box Arithmetic- a leaner foundation to polynomials that are directly encodible on a computer.  It accomplishes this through the ouroboros operators $\circlearrowleft$ and $\circlearrowright$ so that $0 \circlearrowright \infty = \{\}_+$, 

$$0 \circlearrowright \infty = \{\}_+$$

$$ \infty \circlearrowright 0 = \{\}_-$$

And from there we can derive the fundemental identity of MMP

$ \{\}_- \circlearrowright \{\}_+ = \{\{\}\}_+ = 0^0 = 1 $


From this, the paradoxical identity $0^0 \equiv 1$ is not a convention inherited from combinatorics or a simplifying assumption that keeps power series tidy. It derives from an idea that these zeros, what ever they may be, have some sort of stress energy vibratring inside itself yet balanced to the outside.  The $1 + -1 = 0$ is not queiting or serenity, but an equal tension of opposing forces, in MMP we need to draw a sharp distinction between $x +y = 0$ and $x = -y$, the former loses the aspect of stress energy, the latter does without paradoxical motions and reveals the balance. 

When viewing the identity geometrically, we note $x^y=k$ as a geometric, dimension hopping construction. A square is $l^2=A$, a cube $l^3=V$ so by the geometric reasoning going from cube to square is 3 to 2, square to line is 2 to 1, line to point is 1 to 0, than what from -1 to 0 or zero to zero?  We can see geometrically 0^0 is trying to climb a dimension up from the zeroth dimension of no length. What is this idea of climbing from this netherspace- this void this dimension 0 into the physical realm.  MMPs fundemental identity illustrates this idea quite clearly, orientated voids knot through each other, composing the unity within the continuum. Infinities length is balances by the nothingnesses precision and vice versa.


Whether this recovers something the Egyptian priests understood and the Greeks only partially received, or simply discovers something new by looking in an old direction — the pyramid was built to last; perhaps it was also built to be read — we cannot say with certainty. What we can say is that the algebra is simple, the results are sharp, and the questions it opens are worth asking.
