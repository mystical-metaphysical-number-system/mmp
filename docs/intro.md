# The Mystical Metaphysical Number System

<video src="/img/mmp-1.webm" controls muted loop playsinline style={{maxWidth: '100%', width: '100%'}} />

Standard mathematics has a silent convention problem. $0^0$ is indeterminate — except when it isn't, and then we justify the result by pointing at power series and empty products. Pi is transcendental and we move on, even as Buckminster Fuller noted that nature builds no circles. Zeno's paradoxes were never resolved, only reclassified. The real numbers are a magnificent scaffolding, but scaffolding built over a crack: something was smuggled in at the foundation and we have been managing the consequences ever since.

MMP begins by asking what zero actually *is*. Not a placeholder, not an absence — a **pole**. Zero has infinite precision and no length; infinity has infinite length and no precision. They are not opposites, they are partners, and when they meet under the right operator the empty box is not a starting point but a *result*. That reorientation is small. What falls out of it is not: $0^0 = 1$ becomes inevitable rather than conventional, balanced ternary emerges as the natural radix, Bell noncommutativity appears as a direct consequence, and a path opens toward analog computation in which the unit cell is an oriented void rather than a bit.

Whether this recovers something the Egyptian priests understood and the Greeks only partially received, or simply discovers something new by looking in an old direction — the pyramid was built to last; perhaps it was also built to be read — we cannot say with certainty. What we can say is that the algebra is simple, the results are sharp, and the questions it opens are worth asking.
