The cascade​

Everywhere 000^000 appears ambiguously in standard work — combinatorics, power series, limits — MMP says: you have two oriented zeros; check their polarities; the answer is fixed by which met which.

Standard mathematics often justifies 00=10^0 = 100=1 by scaffolding: empty mappings, the empty product, conventions that keep power series tidy. The result is justified by its consequences rather than derived from what zero is.

MMP asks first: what kind of thing is zero? The answer — zero is not mere absence but a pole, an oriented void, one end of the ouroboros — makes 00=10^0 = 100=1 fall out as a categorical statement about the nature of nothingness. Not "this is convenient," but this could not be otherwise.

The ancient intuition that zero and infinity are partners rather than opposites, and that nothingness has direction, is what dissolves the ambiguity without appealing to combinatorics or power series alone: a categorical claim on nothingness, not an identity chosen for downstream convenience.
