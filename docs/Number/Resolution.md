## The Infinite Nothingness

The paradox resolves into two extremal states and a null:

$$
0 \odot \infty = \{\}^+ \quad \text{(frame out — leaving the numberline)} \\
\infty \odot 0 = \{\}^- \quad \text{(frame in — folding the numberline into itself)} \\
\{\}^- \odot \{\}^+ = \{\}
$$

## The Ouroboros Operators

$$
\{\}^+ \odot \{\}^+ = 1 = \{\{\}\}^+ \\
\{\}^- \odot \{\}^- = -1 = \{\{\}\}^-
$$
