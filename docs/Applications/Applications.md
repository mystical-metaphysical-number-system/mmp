# Applications

This section lays out the *why* of rerouting box math — the practical and philosophical payoffs of founding arithmetic on the infinite nothingness rather than the empty box.

The framework bridges the realm of ZFC real-number pureism and the realm of finite computationalist practicalism, and makes explicit the distinction between precision at iteration and absolute exactness through a simple, understandable formalism. The following sections apply that machinery across several domains.
