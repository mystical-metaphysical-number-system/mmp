# ArchaeoArithmetic [Coming Soon]

MMP is motivated by the persuasion that the constructors of the Great Pyramid had a more nuanced understanding of how nature absolves itself of incommensurability — *nature does not use π* (Buckminster Fuller) — and that this understanding was encoded in the pyramid.

We approach ancient mathematics through a different lens. Strabo (64 BCE–25 CE) admitted: *the Egyptian priests are supreme in the science of the sky; they impart some of their precepts although they conceal the greater part; they revealed to the Greeks the secrets of the full year, whom the latter ignored as with many other things.*
